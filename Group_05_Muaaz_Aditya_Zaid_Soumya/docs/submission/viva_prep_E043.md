## Part 1. The five standard viva questions, answered for E043

### Q1. Why that solref and friction, and what if you halved them?

What the files say.
- Timestep 0.002 s in both scenes (models/campus_perimeter_patrol.xml:19, models/campus_perimeter_patrol_exterior.xml:28, integrator implicitfast).
- Global geom default friction "1.0 0.01 0.005", condim 3 (models/common.xml:66). The quad class sets solref "0.004 1" (models/common.xml:72).
- Reasoning (`docs/research/mjcf_parameter_justification.md`): 1.0 is MuJoCo's own default sliding value and holds a landed airframe on the dock; with condim 3 only the sliding coefficient is active, so the 0.01 torsional and 0.005 rolling values are inert. solref = (time constant 0.004 s, damping ratio 1): ratio 1 is critical damping, so the legs settle without bounce.
- In flight the motion is thrust-driven; the airframe touches a surface at the dock and in the rare contact, so these two numbers drive no patrol metric.

What I measured (EV `viva_contact_solref_mix.txt`, `viva_contact_halved.txt`; one deterministic 2 s rest on the dock with rotors off, exterior scene file, NOT a dossier result).
- A leg touching the building or ground does NOT use 0.004. The geom value 0.004 is averaged with the other geom's default 0.02 (solmix 1 and 1), so the contact uses solref [0.012, 1.0], friction 1.0, in both scenes.
- Halving the leg time constant to 0.002 gives deepest penetration 2.124 mm against 2.416 mm shipped; doubling to 0.008 gives 2.979 mm. Rest height is the same (36.1880 m). Halving solref changes penetration by about 0.3 mm and nothing a patrol metric can see.
- Halving friction to 0.5, or cutting it to 0.1, gives 0.000 mm drift at rest. Friction only matters when an airframe lands or slides with lateral speed; that case is NOT measured. Say so if asked.
- The 180-run batch was not re-run with other contact parameters. "What if halved" is answered by the rest test and by reasoning, not by a batch result.
- Correction to know (case E043-C in `ai_audit_candidates.md`): the justification file once said the 0.004 s constant was "silently clamped" at the old 0.004 s timestep. The printed contact value 0.012 s is above 2 x timestep at both timesteps, so no clamp acted; the file was corrected. One sentence in that file still quotes an old head-on separation (3.29 m); do not quote it, the current figure is in Part 5.

### Q4. Why Welch's t-test, and what does Cohen's d mean?

Source for every number here: `analytics/statistical_validation_output.txt` (csv sha256 matches the provenance; alpha 0.05).
- Why Welch: spreads differ by an order of magnitude. cycle_time_s, interior: guard sd 593.2051 against uav_sectors sd 5.9314; exterior 141.6419 against 8.2237. Student's pooled-variance t assumes equal variance; Welch uses se = sqrt(sA^2/nA + sB^2/nB) and Satterthwaite df (analytics/statistical_validation.py:137-157 `welch`).
- PRIMARY (cycle_time_s, guard_baseline vs uav_sectors, n = 30 each): interior t = 22.1870, df 29.006, p 9.629081e-20, d 5.7286, 95 % CI of the difference [2181.5396, 2624.5707] s. Exterior t = 17.8000, df 29.196, p 3.223302e-17, d 4.5959, CI [408.1221, 514.0494] s.
- Cohen's d = mean difference / pooled sd; 5.7 means the means are 5.7 pooled standard deviations apart (0.8 is conventionally "large"). The p value only says equal means are very unlikely; d and the CI say how big.
- Secondary uav_sectors vs uav_offset_ring, cycle time: interior d 4.3299, exterior d 4.9292, both p below 1e-18. The equal-window revisit is not tested for any pair (right-censored guard).
- Caveats to volunteer: (1) the guard's equal-window worst revisit is right-censored (>= 300 s; 30 of 30 guard runs had no revisit inside 300 s, per scene), so no t-test, d or CI is computed on it; the UAV worst revisit max is 108.65 s interior and 133.63 s exterior, and 0 of 30 runs in every UAV cell are censored; (2) seeds are shared across conditions (paired design), the script treats runs as independent and a paired test was not run (not measured); (3) the guard windows (5000 s interior, 1800 s exterior) differ from the UAV window (300 s), which is why the primary metric is cycle_time_s (window-independent) and the revisit comparison uses one common window; (4) the exterior numbers belong to the 22 m layer chosen after the interim failure (Part 0), so they are not comparable with any earlier exterior figure; (5) simulation, so p says the simulator separates the conditions, not that a real campus would.

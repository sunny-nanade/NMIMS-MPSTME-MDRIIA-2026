# Peer review template (dossier Section 5.2)

Source: Showcase Guide section 9. Group 05 reviews **Group 06** and **Group 11**. Score each group's LinkedIn video 0-2 on five criteria, then give one best moment and one viva question.
Each member fills this for themselves in the dossier. This file is a blank working sheet: it holds no scores, names or URLs for anyone, and nothing is to be filled in here on anyone else's behalf. The scores you give go into your own copy and into your own dossier Section 5.2.
Watch the video with the sound on. Score only what you can see or hear in it. If something is not shown, score it 0 or 1 and say what was missing.

Scale used on both sheets (our wording, the Guide gives only the range 0-2): 0 = missing or wrong, 1 = present but partial or unclear, 2 = clear and convincing.

## Rules for us

- Be specific and kind. Quote what you saw, not what you assume they did.
- Do not copy a score from a teammate: Section 5.2 is each member's own review. Each of the four members fills their own pair of sheets.
- Peer-review scores are never written into any shared file in the repository by anyone but the reviewer; the video URLs you review are copied by you from the posts themselves.
- Both videos must be posted before the deadline for review; if a video is not posted yet, leave its sheet blank, note in the group chat that it is not up, and come back; never score a video you have not watched, and never estimate a score in advance.

Reproduction on the same 58 interior noon frames (EV `viva_e051_satfrac_repro.txt`, script `viva_e051_satfrac_repro.py`, nothing written):
- HSV_SAT_FRAC 0.66: mean IoU 0.874, hit rate 0.862, worst frame IoU 0.000.
- HSV_SAT_FRAC 0.75: mean IoU 0.985, hit rate 1.000, worst frame IoU 0.939.

## Part 2. Questions on your own code (answers with file:line)

1. Why two hue bands? OpenCV hue is 0..179 (2 degrees per unit); the intruder RGB (0.90, 0.10, 0.10) = (230, 26, 26) has H = 0, S = 226, V = 230, so red sits at the wrap of the hue circle. `_hsv_bands` (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:816-826) builds [0, 6] and the wrap band [174, 179] from `INTRUDER_RGB` (line 793) and `HSV_HUE_TOL` 6 (line 794); test tests/test_e051_vision.py:97 `test_hsv_bands_come_from_the_intruder_colour`.
2. Why is the hue tolerance only 6 units? The orange lobby floor sits at about 9 units (comment src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:794); wider would admit the floor.
3. Why V_min 40 and what is the low-light gain? `HSV_VAL_MIN` 40 (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:797) is the sensor-noise floor and decides twilight. The gain (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:839-844) multiplies the V channel by clip(`AE_TARGET_V` 200 / 99th percentile of V, 1, `AE_GAIN_MAX` 8), hue and saturation untouched, so normal light has gain 1 (constants lines 803-805). Fixture test tests/test_e051_vision.py:82 `test_fixture_low_light_gain_recovers_a_dim_target_and_leaves_normal_light_alone`; ablation off arm in `analytics/vision_illumination_summary.txt`.
4. Why open then close, and why RGB not BGR? `COLOR_RGB2HSV` at src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:838 because the MuJoCo renderer returns RGB; open 3x3 (`MORPH_OPEN`, src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:848) removes speckle (test `test_fixture_speckle_is_removed_and_largest_blob_wins` at tests/test_e051_vision.py:62), close 5x5 (`MORPH_CLOSE`, src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:849) fills holes; the largest external contour wins (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:853); bbox is `(x, y, x + w, y + h)`; the centroid is from moments.
5. Derive the pinhole focal length. `focal_length_px` (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:811-813): f = (H/2) / tan(fovy/2) = 240 / tan(28.07 deg) = 449.9, so 450 (default of `inverse_pinhole_projection`, line 866); fovy 56.14 deg (`CAM_FOVY_DEG`, line 790); test tests/test_e051_vision.py:106 `test_focal_length_from_fovy_is_450px`.
6. Nadir closed form and camera-ray form? `inverse_pinhole_projection` (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:866-892). Nadir: X = X_uav + (u - 320) h / f, Y = Y_uav - (v - 240) h / f (`focal_length_px`, src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:881-883, test `test_fixture_nadir_closed_form` at tests/test_e051_vision.py:110). With the camera pose: d_cam = ((u - u0)/f, -(v - v0)/f, -1), the world ray `d_w` = R d_cam (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:886), intersect the plane z = ground_z at t = (ground_z - C_z) / d_w_z (`ground_z`, src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:889); returns None at or above the horizon (`d_w`, src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:887-888, test `test_camera_ray_above_horizon_returns_none` at tests/test_e051_vision.py:144); round trip test `:122` `test_fixture_camera_ray_roundtrip_oblique`.
7. Why ground_z = 0.85 m? The blob centroid is the middle of a 1.7 m person; intersecting at the floor overshoots (`ASSUMED_TARGET_CENTROID_Z`, src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:806, ASSUMPTION; used at src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:1268). Measured projection error: interior noon mean 0.470 m (p95 1.537), exterior noon 0.062 m (p95 0.105) (`analytics/vision_illumination_summary.txt`; bound test tests/test_e051_vision.py:174 `test_projection_error_bound`).
8. How does handoff avoid ping-pong? `select_tracking_uav` (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:913-938): among UAVs whose camera holds the target (`_target_in_view`, src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:894-911, `HANDOFF_EDGE_PX` 40) pick the nearest; keep the current tracker unless another is more than `HANDOFF_HYST_M` 2.0 m closer (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:935, constant line 807). Tests in tests/test_e051_vision.py: line 187 (target follows), line 193 (hysteresis prevents ping-pong), line 216 (nobody in view picks the nearest); the FSM test through the real function is `test_g03_select_tracking_uav_drives_alert_to_track_without_monkeypatching` at tests/test_e043_surveillance_fsm.py:152.
9. How do you know the detector uses only the image? tests/test_e051_vision.py:72 `test_detector_reads_no_simulator_state` walks the function's AST and runs it on an object with no model or data; only the five constants and `_hsv_bands` are allowed.
10. How is the benchmark ground truth made and which frames are skipped? Segmentation rendering of the intruder geom (analytics/vision_illumination_benchmark.py:174 `enable_segmentation_rendering`; `gt_source` in its provenance); frames with a mask under `MIN_GT_PIXELS` 50 are skipped (analytics/vision_illumination_benchmark.py:48, applied at `:220`): 6 interior and 28 exterior frames (header of `analytics/vision_illumination_summary.txt`).
11. How was twilight defined? Headlight and every scene light scaled by 0.15 (analytics/vision_illumination_benchmark.py:11-12; the ablation runner uses the same rule, analytics/run_benchmark.py:107 `scale_lighting`). This is an ASSUMPTION, not a measured lux.
12. What is the YOLO part? analytics/vision_yolo_compare.py:31 `CONF` 0.25, class-agnostic best box on the same frames, yolov8n.pt COCO-pretrained, CPU, run in a separate environment. It is optional and secondary; OpenCV HSV is primary. Keep the AGPL-3.0 note if YOLO is shown.
13. Why does the exterior produce 5 false alarms per run? A false alarm is a verified incident that is not the intruder. The exterior scene has five parked tempos with a red cab material `tempo_cab` (models/nmims_main_campus.xml:238 and four more bodies; material models/common.xml:23) in the same hue band; the count is exactly 5.00 per run in both exterior UAV cells (EV `viva_false_alarms.txt`). That the five cabs are the five alarms is consistent with the counts, but the per-run object was not itemised. A colour detector cannot separate them from the intruder; the FSM's static-handover rule (src/aerial_patrol_swarm.py:1454-1461) frees the UAV after 5 s but the alarm is still counted.

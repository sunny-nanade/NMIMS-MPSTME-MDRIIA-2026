### Q4. Why Welch's t-test, and what does Cohen's d mean?

Source: `analytics/statistical_validation_output.txt`. Welch because the spreads differ by an order of magnitude: cycle_time_s interior guard sd 593.2051 against uav_sectors sd 5.9314; exterior 141.6419 against 8.2237. Primary (guard_baseline vs uav_sectors, cycle_time_s, n = 30 each): interior t = 22.1870, df 29.006, p 9.629081e-20, d 5.7286, 95 % CI of the mean difference [2181.5396, 2624.5707] s; exterior t = 17.8000, df 29.196, p 3.223302e-17, d 4.5959, CI [408.1221, 514.0494] s. d = mean difference / pooled sd (about 5.7 pooled standard deviations apart interior). Two interval conventions exist: your script uses a t interval for the mean, `t_ci` (analytics/campus_security_economics.py:106-113), for example guard interior cycle time 2460.46 s (95 % t-CI 2238.96 to 2681.97) in your output; E075's script uses the t distribution for the difference of means.

1Executive Summary

MPSTME GUARDIAN simulates two cooperating quadrotors patrolling the real perimeter of the NMIMS Main Building in Vile Parle (West), and measures — rather than asserts — how much faster they revisit every point on that perimeter, and how quickly they detect an intruder, than a guard on foot. The repository now follows the course scaffold exactly: one MJCF model, one control file with a TODO region per student, and an analytics folder whose benchmark CSV feeds the 4-page IEEE manuscript.

±0.03 maltitude hold in gusts
41.0 sworst revisit, offset ring

Everything in that row is measured in MuJoCo on the generated NMIMS scene. Three further results from this revision change what the team must build:

  • The guide’s APF does not guarantee separation at cruise speed. In a measured head-on pass at 6 m/s per aircraft, APF alone (krep = 8.5, dsafe = 2.5 m) let the centres reach 0.40 m — contact. A layered scheme (altitude layers + closing-speed governor + right-hand sidestep, APF kept as the inner layer) holds 3.29 m and the pair passes cleanly.
  • The level-camera pinhole formula is wrong by metres in flight. Measured tilt during cruise is 7.2° median and 28.5° at p95, which displaces the projected ground point by 2.5–3.0 m median and 11–13 m at p95 — wider than the 5 m geofence warning buffer.
  • The scaffold’s figure generator fabricates the benchmark CSV from np.random.normal. It has been rewritten to read data only and to refuse any CSV that lacks a provenance record. The scaffold CSV is preserved untouched as a placeholder.

11Experimental Design

FactorLevelsWhy
Coordination policysectors (guide) · offset ringIsolates the policy effect — the paper’s contribution
Seed≥ 80, shared across policiesPairs trials, so a paired t-test is legitimate (blueprint specifies paired)
Windmean 0–6 m/s, OU gustsRejects the “calm air only” criticism
Intrusion timeuniform 40–160 s per seedLatency must measure the system, not one lucky geometry
Illumination1000 · 250 · 50 · 5 lx tiersGuide’s latency/IoU-versus-light analysis

Statistics: paired two-tailed t-test per metric, with mean difference, 95 % CI and effect size; IoU reported as median and IQR; every plotted mean carries a standard-error bar and its n.

12CSBS Dimensionless Economics

The course forbids currency. The guide’s structure is kept; its inputs change from typed constants to measurements and sourced ratios:

S_patrol   = guard_cycle_min / uav_cycle_min                       (both from the CSV)
eta_labor  = H_reallocated / H_total                               (NMIMS roster; E077)
dOpEx      = 1 − C_hybrid / C_guard-only     with C_guard-only ≡ 1.00
P_payback  = K_CapEx,normalised / dOpEx      reported across a band of C_hybrid and K
The comparator decides the headline A guard walking the 342 m perimeter needs about 5 minutes; a guard completing a full round of a ten-storey building needs far longer. Spatrol can be 7× or 70× depending on which is chosen. E077 must fix the definition first, in writing, and the paper must state it.

14Integrity, Licensing, Privacy

  • COPE. No number reaches the paper unless run_benchmark.py produced it with provenance.
  • OpenStreetMap. Geometry is ODbL 1.0; the attribution “© OpenStreetMap contributors” must appear in the paper and the README.
  • NMIMS imagery. The tour panorama is NMIMS’s photograph. It is used only for the side-by-side validation figure, with credit; confirm with faculty before including it in the public LinkedIn video.
  • Privacy. Detection is person-presence only — no identity, no faces, no biometric storage.
  • Deployment. Simulation only. Any physical flight over Vile Parle would fall under Indian civil UAS rules and the airport-vicinity airspace map; nothing here is a compliance determination.

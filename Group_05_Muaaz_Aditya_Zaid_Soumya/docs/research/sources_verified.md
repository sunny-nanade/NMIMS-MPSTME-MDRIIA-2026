# Sources verified (research-sources, 29 Oct 2026; standards re-verified 30 Oct 2026, see docs/research/standards_verified.md)

## How verified
Pages located with WebSearch and read with WebFetch or curl. Quotes are taken from the page or gazette text actually retrieved. ISO, IEC, RIA and ASTM standard bodies return HTTP 403 or sit behind a paywall, so for those the quote comes from store or catalogue pages (the fetch tool returns a model-written summary of the page, flagged per row) and the clause number is 'not verifiable from the public scope page'. The Drone Rules text was read from the official e-gazette PDF. No figure below is estimated; anything without a primary source is labelled ASSUMPTION with a range. The Standards Manual excerpt (SP/standards_manual.md) is a course document, not a primary source.

## L-23 update (30 Oct 2026, G-19)

Full re-check in `docs/research/standards_verified.md`. Changes to the rows above: (1) ISO 10218-1:2011 was withdrawn and replaced by ISO 10218-1:2025 (5 Feb 2025), whose scope lists "airborne and space robots" among non-applicable uses (https://genorma.com/en/standards/iso-10218-1-2025); (2) ANSI/RIA R15.06-2012 is now ANSI/A3 R15.06-2025, the US adoption of ISO 10218 Parts 1 and 2 (https://www.therobotreport.com/updated-ansi-a3-standards-address-industrial-robot-safety/); (3) IEC 60601-1 quote confirmed on the official IEC webstore (https://webstore.iec.ch/publication/2612); (4) ISO 21384-3:2023 is withdrawn, replaced by ISO 21384-3:2026 published 7 Aug 2026 (https://genorma.com/en/standards/iso-21384-3-2026); (5) ASTM F3322 scope is clause 1.1 of F3322-24a on the official ASTM store (https://store.astm.org/f3322-24a.html); (6) India Drone Rules: decision "Partly" with rule numbers 2(1)(c), 14(1), 21, 22, 42 read from the gazette text. iso.org, webstore.ansi.org and the A3 site returned HTTP 403, so ISO and A3 rows rest on catalogue pages.

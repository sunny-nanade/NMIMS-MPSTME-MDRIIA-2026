Where the simulation fits

Tremor is a small slice of a roughly 1 mm error budget

The whole-system radial error of DBS is about 1.06 mm (Giridharan 2022; 1.01 to 1.32 mm in a Barcelona cohort, 2025). Our MuJoCo study measures the hand-tremor part of that, and nothing else.

Because the filter's share is under 1 % of the budget, we make no claim that it changes revision rates, outcomes or cost. The business case above does not use it.
Robotics evidence

Which filter actually helps the hand hold still

Enhanced-tremor hands, 30 paired MuJoCo trials per method, tuned position loop (KP 50). Hold error is the tip's RMS error while holding at the target. Tracking error is measured while inserting. Methods that win hold error by lagging the hand pay for it in tracking.

Drift is the real floor. With measured-scale drift every method sits at about 0.28 mm hold error, and no tremor filter touches it.
Feedback delay matters more than the filter: at the original gains the loop is stable to about 2 ms and diverges from 6 ms. Real latency must be measured before any hardware claim.

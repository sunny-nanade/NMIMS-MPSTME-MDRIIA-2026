## 1. Mathematical and Aerodynamic Formulation

### 1.1 Multirotor Flight & Slung-Load Equations of Motion
The aerial vehicle has mass $m_q$, inertia matrix $I_q \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$, position $p_q = [x_q, y_q, z_q]^T$, and attitude rotation matrix $R \in SO(3)$. A payload of mass $m_p$ is suspended via a cable of length $L$ with directional unit vector $p_L$:

$$(m_q + m_p) \ddot{p}_q - m_p L (\ddot{p}_L) = \sum_{i=1}^{4} T_i R e_3 - (m_q + m_p) g e_3 + F_{\text{wind}}$$

where $T_i = c_T \omega_i^2$ is the aerodynamic thrust generated by rotor $i$, and $F_{\text{wind}}$ is the turbulent aerodynamic drag modeled via the crosswind velocity vector $v_w$:

$$F_{\text{drag}} = \frac{1}{2} \rho C_d A \|v_q - v_w\| (v_q - v_w)$$

The payload pendulum angular dynamics in spherical coordinates $(\theta_L, \phi_L)$ satisfy:

$$\ddot{\theta}_L + \frac{g}{L} \sin \theta_L = -\frac{\ddot{x}_q}{L} \cos \theta_L$$

### 1.2 Payload-Range Analytical Energy Model
Following Dorling et al. (2017), electrical power consumption $P(m_p, v)$ during level cruise flight at airspeed $v$ carrying payload $m_p$ is:

$$P(m_p, v) = \frac{(m_q + m_{\text{bat}} + m_p)^{3/2}}{\sqrt{2 \rho A_{\text{disk}}}} + \frac{1}{2} C_{\text{drag}} \rho v^3 A_{\text{front}} + P_{\text{avionics}}$$

The maximum operational mission radius $R_{\max}$ given battery energy capacity $E_{\text{batt}}$ (in Joules) with a 20% emergency reserve is:

$$R_{\max}(m_p) = \frac{0.80 \cdot E_{\text{batt}}}{2 \cdot P(m_p, v_{\text{cruise}})} \cdot v_{\text{cruise}}$$

### 1.3 Circular Error Probable (CEP) Drop Accuracy
Payload impact positions $(x_i, y_i)$ relative to target center $(0, 0)$ yield radial radial displacement $r_i = \sqrt{x_i^2 + y_i^2}$. The Circular Error Probable (CEP50) represents the radius of the circle enclosing 50% of relief drops:

$$\text{CEP}_{50} = 0.589 (\sigma_x + \sigma_y)$$

$$\text{CEP}_{95} = 2.08 \cdot \text{CEP}_{50}$$

### 1.4 CSBS Disaster Relief Economic Parity
To assess logistics economics without arbitrary currency values, operational cost is formulated as dimensionless cost parity $\kappa$:

$$\kappa = \frac{\text{OpEx}_{\text{uav\_fleet}}}{\text{OpEx}_{\text{boat\_convoy}}} = \frac{C_{\text{energy}} + C_{\text{maintenance}} + C_{\text{pilot\_ops}}}{C_{\text{boat\_fuel}} + C_{\text{marine\_crew}}}$$

The dimensionless amortization payback horizon in operational months is:

$$\text{Payback Months} = \frac{K_{\text{capex}}}{1 - \kappa} \times 12$$
